Card Puzzles Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 28, 2026

This policy explains how Card Puzzles handles information when you use the app or contact its developer. For privacy questions, email contact.cardpuzzles@gmail.com.

Playing the game

You do not need a Card Puzzles account to play. Card Puzzles does not operate its own server for player accounts or gameplay, and does not automatically send your puzzle progress to the developer. The app does not include advertising or third-party analytics SDKs, and does not sell your personal information.

Information stored on your device

The app saves information needed to provide its features, including solved puzzles, favorites, unfinished games, progress and achievements, settings, purchase-access information, and whether certain one-time messages have been shown. This information is stored in the app's local storage.

Your operating system may include app data in device backups or transfers, depending on your device settings and platform services. Such backups are handled by the platform provider, rather than by a Card Puzzles server.

Optional purchases

Optional puzzle-pack purchases are processed by Google Play on Android and Apple's App Store on iOS. The app receives store-provided purchase information, such as product identifiers, transaction or purchase tokens, and ownership status, to unlock packs, restore purchases, and respond to refunds or revocations. It may store purchase-access information locally.

The developer does not receive your payment-card details through the app. Google and Apple process payment, account, device, and other information under their own policies. See Google's Privacy Policy and Apple's Privacy Policy.

Support and problem reports

If you choose Report a problem, the app prepares an email or share draft containing your description, the app version, puzzle details, and the current card position and play history. You can review the draft before sending it. The app does not send the report automatically.

If you send a report or contact support, the developer receives your message, your email address, and any information or attachments you include. This information is used to respond to you, investigate problems, and improve the app. Email and attachments are processed by the email providers used to send and receive them.

Export, import, and sharing

If you export progress, the app writes puzzle identifiers, solved status, and favorites to a file you choose. If you import progress, the app reads the file you select. Files saved to a cloud-storage provider are subject to that provider's policies.

If you share a puzzle, the app prepares text and a link containing the card deal and solution for the destination you select. Shared Handviewer links open a third-party website. Reference links and app-store links also open external services. Those services handle information according to their own policies.

Retention and deletion

Local game data remains until it is changed or removed. On Android, you can remove the app's local data through device Settings by clearing its storage. Removing the app generally removes its local data, although operating-system backups may restore information when it is reinstalled. Exported files and device backups must be deleted separately using the relevant file or backup service. Clearing app data does not cancel or refund a store purchase.

Support messages are retained for as long as needed to resolve the inquiry, investigate related issues, and meet applicable recordkeeping obligations. You can request deletion of support correspondence by emailing contact.cardpuzzles@gmail.com. Purchase records held by Google or Apple are managed by those companies.

Security

The app uses platform-provided local storage and purchase services. Support reports and shared files leave the app only when you choose to send or save them. No method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed completely secure.

Children's information

The app does not ask players to provide a name, age, email address, or other personal profile information to play. If you believe a child has provided personal information through a support message, contact the developer to request its deletion.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app's features or information-handling practices change. The effective date above identifies the current version.
